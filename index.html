How it works

1 · ExportLast month's reservations from FBS, iLab or PPMS and the instrument-PC login log. No IT ticket, no install.
2 · CheckLaBro reconciles them in your browser. For instruments with no PC (centrifuges, shop tools), a clip-on current sensor records run time.
3 · SendA charges file for FBS, PI statements, a rate file. Every number traces back to a booking, a login or a run.

Pilot

Send one month of exports. Within 48 hours you get checked charges, a PI statement and a rate check. Free for the first month, then $200 a month per core.

  • In the web app, your files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
