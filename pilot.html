Month-end billing and recharge rates, built on the hours your instruments actually ran.

For university core facilities · 2026

The problem

  • Cores bill by bookings or PC logins; neither is what the instrument actually ran. In a sample month, booking and login billing for one 5-instrument core differ by $5,646 (synthetic data).
  • Managers reconcile FBS/iLab/PPMS exports against login logs by hand every month, and answer PI disputes without evidence.
  • Next year's recharge rate is cost ÷ hours. Federal rules (2 CFR 200.468) want rates based on actual usage; auditors still question charges that can't show it (e.g. NSF OIG, University of Nebraska–Lincoln, 2025: $92,865 inadequately supported service-center expenses).

Rate check

Next year's rate from measured hours; surplus/deficit; rows for your finance office's rate template.
