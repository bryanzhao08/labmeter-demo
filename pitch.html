How LabMeter works

1 "LabMeter is a clip-on sensor on the instrument's power cord. It sees when the machine actually runs, even with no computer attached. Nothing gets opened or rewired."
2 "It sends that wirelessly to a small gateway in the room…"
3 "…to our software, which applies the core's own rules: grace windows, penalties, no-shows…"
4 "…alongside the files the core already has: bookings and logins."
5 "Every user, every day, gets a charge with a reason. One click sends the corrected charges back to FBS."
