Download Parity 0.8.1

Matches lab bookings to real instrument use and drafts the bill.

Mac (Apple silicon)Windows (preview, not yet verified)

First launch

If macOS says “Apple could not verify Parity”, click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password. You only do this once.

Or paste this in Terminal after moving Parity to Applications:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Parity.app

If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose More info › Run anyway.

Try it in 2 minutes

  1. Open Parity.
  2. Choose Try the scheduling agent.
  3. Pick FBS practice.
  4. Select Run.
