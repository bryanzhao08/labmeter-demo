Entry 02 / A better way to close the month

Bring it together.
Check it once.

Give LaBro the bookings from your scheduling software, the usage its instruments record, and the prices your lab already uses.

It lines up the records, applies your billing rules and flags the sessions that need a look. The manager reviews before exporting charges.

Less copy and paste. More context.

A month in three steps

  1. Collect your recordsBookings from your scheduler (FBS, iLab or PPMS). Usage from power-cord sensors and instrument-computer logins. Your price sheet.
  2. Check the differencesSee overruns, missing bookings and records that don't line up.
  3. Review and exportKeep the decision with the person who knows the lab.
Where do the agents fit?

The preview's scheduling agent collects and validates bookings from FBS and iLab practice systems. Real schedules currently use exports. Claude and Codex can run LaBro's local checks through MCP.
