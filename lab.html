    Now try your own core

    From first principles: how much use goes unbilled, and how much time the monthly audit takes. Defaults are a typical 15-instrument core; change them to match yours.

    • Rates: published internal self-service rates run $20–150/h (UMass, Clemson, OHSU, Yale, UNMC). $60 is a mid mix.
    • Hours: UChicago's flow core logged 14,295 h on 24 instruments, about 600 h each.
    • Billed wrong: when a UNC microscopy core switched to measured time, billable hours went from ~1,000 to ~775 a month with the same number of reservations (Clissold & Itano, BioTechniques 2025). 8% is our conservative estimate; no study measures this nationally.
    • Manager cost: median core manager salary $92,147 (ABRF 2022), about $58/h with benefits. Hours on billing are our estimate.

    Simulation settings and assumptions
    • All people, labs and sessions are simulated. Rates match the demo catalog ($25–60/h).
    • Bill the booking: reserved time is charged, no-shows charged in full, overruns and walk-ups are missed.
    • Bill the login: computer session time is charged, including data transfer and forgotten logouts. Instruments with no computer fall back to the booking.
    • LaBro: actual run time; past the grace window and unbooked use are charged at 2× (the core's penalty); no-shows charged in full.
    • Manager time by hand: 2 minutes per session to compare files plus 10 minutes per exception. With LaBro: 1.5 minutes to approve each exception. These are estimates to replace with a real core's numbers.
    • The first day is scripted for the guided tour; the rest is random with a fixed seed.
